About

Sharma & Vishnu Fast Food is a popular, expanding local chain in Bhopal, India, with over 15 outlets offering vegetarian, North Indian, Chinese, and fast food, including sandwiches, paneer kulcha, and tea. Known for consistent, affordable, and quick service, it is highly rated for casual dining, with popular locations including Arera Colony, Lalghati, and Hoshangabad Road.
